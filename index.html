How the Q4 no-live dates were selected

1. Remove campaigns

Every supplied TikTok and Shopee campaign date remains a mandatory live date.

2. Rank normal weekdays

Tuesday is removed first, then Monday. Thursday is used only when a month lacks enough Tuesday/Monday options.

3. Check date evidence

Protect strong recurring normal dates where possible. Employee requests only break close ties.

What management should monitor

  • Traffic: views/hour and impressions/hour. September shows that low traffic can reduce GMV/hour even when GMV per visitor changes only moderately.
  • Monetization: GMV/view, product clicks, CTOR, AOV and paid orders.
  • Commercial quality: net GMV/hour after cancellations, returns, vouchers, platform fees and host cost.
  • Reliability: median, number of sessions and number of observed dates beside every average.
  • Data limitation: Views is used as the visitor proxy because the export does not provide confirmed unique visitors.

Data basis

  • Source: Performance Analysis R1.csv, Warrix channel, 1 June–17 September 2026.
  • 183 valid GMV/hour sessions across 104 dates.
  • Campaign classification: union of all supplied platform campaign calendars.
  • Zeros are retained as real zero-GMV sessions; null GMV/hour values are excluded.
  • No start-time or time-slot analysis is included in this report.
